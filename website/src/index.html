Dev servers stay up. Ports come home.

Tick Keep running, or run a dev, start or serve script, and the server stays up on the Mac until you press Stop, even after the PC disconnects. Its ports open at the same address on your PC.

  • Only ports the server itself listens on are forwarded. Nothing else on the Mac is reachable.
  • The Mac stays awake while a server runs, and the ports reconnect quietly on flaky networks.

Sealed like a chip package.

A job is your code plus every package it installs, so the Mac treats it as untrusted. Each one runs in a macOS sandbox, in a fresh workspace that is deleted when it ends.

  • Its own workspaceRead and write
  • Your toolchainsHomebrew · rustup · nvm · pyenv · XcodeRead
  • Downloads and its own serversAllowed
  • The rest of your home folder~/Documents · ~/.ssh · ~/.awsBlocked
  • Clipboard, keychain, other apps' settingsBlocked
  • Your ssh agent and local databasesPostgreSQL · Redis · Docker · OllamaBlocked
  • Opening apps on the MacBlocked

The Mac stays yours.

  • Only your own account's devices can ask to pair, and only after you approve a code shown on both screens.
  • Devices talk over encrypted QUIC with their own keys. Relays forward traffic they cannot read.
  • Every run, approval and paired device is listed on the Mac. Remove a device and its new jobs stop within 15 seconds.
  • Updates install only when signed with the project's key.
