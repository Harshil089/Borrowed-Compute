One app, on every computer you own. Borrowed Compute runs your code on your other computers and lends this one to them: Mac or PC, either way. Both links always give the newest release, and the app keeps itself up to date.

For your Mac

Borrowed Compute

Versionlatest
Needs
A Mac with Apple Silicon
File
BorrowedComputeAgent.dmg
  1. Open the disk image and drag the app to Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS blocks it because it is not notarized yet: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Sign in. On your first computer, choose Keep the account on this computer; on the others, pick it from your network. A Mac lends itself from the start.

For your PC

Borrowed Compute

Versionlatest
Needs
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
File
BorrowedCompute-setup.exe
  1. Run the installer. If SmartScreen warns, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Sign in. Pick the computer your account lives on from your network, paste its code, or keep the account on this PC.
  3. To run code elsewhere, click Pair next to a computer and type the code on it. To lend this PC, turn on Lend this device under Overview.

Already installed?

Nothing to do. Borrowed Compute Agent on a Mac and Borrowed Compute on a PC update themselves into this one app, keeping your sign-in and paired devices. It installs a newer release when it starts, and Check for updates does it on demand. Every update is verified against the project's signing key first.
