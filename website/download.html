One app, on every
computer you own.

It runs your code on your other computers and lends this one to them, Mac or PC, either way. Both links always give the newest release, and the app keeps itself up to date.

For your Mac

Apple Silicon · BorrowedComputeAgent.dmg

latest
  1. Open the disk image and drag the app to Applications.
  2. The first time, macOS blocks it because it is not notarized yet: open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Sign in. On your first computer, choose Keep the account on this computer; on the others, pick it from your network. A Mac lends itself from the start.
Download for Mac macOS won't open it?

For your PC

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · BorrowedCompute-setup.exe

latest
  1. Run the installer. If SmartScreen warns, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Sign in. Pick the computer your account lives on from your network, paste its code, or keep the account on this PC.
  3. To run code elsewhere, click Pair next to a computer and type the code on it. To lend this PC, turn on Lend this device under Overview.
Download for Windows Includes the bc command line

Already installed?

Nothing to do. The app installs a newer release when it starts, keeping your sign-in and paired devices, and Check for updates does it on demand. Every update is verified against the project's signing key first.
