Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The work runs on your own Mac and the connection goes between your own devices, so there is no compute or storage for us to pay for and pass on.

Do I need an account?

You sign in with GitHub or Google on both machines so they can find each other. Your Mac hosts the sign-in itself; there is no separate Borrowed Compute account to create.

Does it use a relay?

Connections go direct when the network allows. On strict networks they fall back to a public relay that forwards encrypted traffic it cannot read.

What happens to my code on the Mac?

It is unpacked into a fresh workspace, run in a sandbox, and deleted with every downloaded dependency when the job ends.

Which Macs work?

Macs with Apple Silicon. The PC app runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11.

Will there be paid plans?

Pricing for teams is not decided. Nothing is planned that would take away what personal use does today.
